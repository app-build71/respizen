Daily Rituals

Adapt RespiZen to Every Moment of Your Day

5s — 5s

Cardiac Coherence (Anti-Stress)

Inhale for 5 seconds, exhale for 5 seconds (6 breaths per minute) for 5 minutes to balance your nervous system and rapidly lower cortisol and daily stress.

4s — 7s — 8s

4-7-8 Deep Relaxation & Sleep

Inhale for 4 seconds, hold gently for 7 seconds, and exhale slowly for 8 seconds. Ideal before bedtime to calm racing thoughts and fall asleep faster.

4s — 4s — 4s — 4s

Square / Box Breathing (Focus)

Equal phases of inhale, hold, exhale, and empty pause. Used by athletes and professionals to regain mental clarity, composure, and focus under pressure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is RespiZen really free? ▼

Yes! RespiZen is completely free to download and use on Android. You can customize all breathing phases, audio ticks, and access your statistics freely.

Do I need an internet connection or an account? ▼

No account is needed and RespiZen works offline. All your preferences and session history remain stored locally on your device—nothing is sent to external servers.

Can I practice without looking at my phone screen? ▼

Absolutely. RespiZen emits a gentle tick every second during inhale and exhale phases, along with distinct gongs when switching phases, allowing you to keep your eyes closed.

Privacy Policy — RespiZen

RespiZen is built with a strict privacy-by-design approach. The application does not require any user account, login, or personal information to function.

All your custom breathing rhythms, audio settings, and session statistics are stored exclusively locally on your Android device. No practice data is ever transmitted to or stored on any RespiZen server.
